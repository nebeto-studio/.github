# Code of Conduct

Nebeto Studio is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming, inclusive, and constructive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and provide actionable feedback in code reviews.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the software.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported confidentially to **contact@nebeto.xyz**. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly.
